# Live eval, 16 Nov 2026: automated scores and a hand audit of every failure

Three live CI runs of the assistant on the real backend (city hotel sample, "today" = Thu 1 Jun 2017),
50 questions each from `evals/questions.yaml`. The automated scorer (`evals/scoring.py`) is strict on
purpose: every number in an answer must trace to a tool output for the day being talked about. This
report checks every failure it raised by hand and says whether the model really got something wrong.

Full CI logs: `evals/reports/logs/2026-11-16_haiku_v1.txt`, `2026-11-16_haiku_v2.txt`,
`2026-11-16_sonnet_v2.txt`.

## How the audit was done

For each failed question, the printed answer was compared with the tool outputs recomputed with
`agent.real_backend.RealBackend()` and `agent.contract.run_tool` (today Thu 1 Jun 2017). Verdicts:

- **real mistake**: the answer is wrong, misleading, breaks a prompt rule, or has a text defect.
- **scorer false alarm**: the answer is correct; the scorer misread it.
- **unclear**: defensible either way; the reason is given.

Only failed answers were checked by hand. Passing answers can still contain mistakes the scorer
missed (see "Scorer misses" below), so the audited counts are a lower bound on real mistakes.

Reference values used most often: `get_flagged_days` 2–28 Jun returns **12** days (busy: 5, 11, 12, 17,
20, 25, 27, 28 Jun; quiet: 7, 10, 16, 23 Jun), threshold 15%. For 1–14 Jun it returns **5** (busy 5, 11,
12; quiet 7, 10). Sat 24 Jun is −14.5% (not flagged). `get_accuracy`: range_percentile 80,
range_meaning "8 in 10 past days stayed within this distance of the forecast", 14-day mean error
10.6, range half-width 16.0, after-export inside_range_pct 64, mean error 8.9.

## Summary of the audit

| run | automated passed | failed answers | real mistake | wrong/invented number | unclear | false alarm only |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Haiku v1 (earlier scorer) | 28/50 | 22 | 5 | 1 | 2 | 15 |
| Haiku v2 | 43/50 | 7 | 4 | 1 | 0 | 3 |
| Sonnet v2 | 39/50 | 11 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 7 |

The most common real mistakes across the three runs:

1. **Wrong count of flagged days** (4 answers): Haiku v1 dr07 ("three quiet days", 1 flagged), Haiku v2 dr02 (13 for 12),
   Sonnet dr02 (11 for 12), Sonnet rd06 ("four", then five). The per-day numbers were right every time; only the
   count was wrong.
2. **Wrong language** (4 answers, all Haiku): English questions answered fully or partly in Dutch (v1 st06,
   rd04, rd07; v2 st07).
3. **Garbled words** (2 answers, Haiku): "минимaal" (v1 nl03), "nanaast" (v2 nl05).

## What changed v1 → v2

Prompt v2 adds two rules to v1: **rule 9**, quote percentages exactly as the tool gives them, with one
decimal and never rounded, so a day never sounds flagged when it isn't; and **rule 10**, answer in
the language the question is written in, with English as the default when unsure. Among Haiku's printed failed
answers, v1 rounded percentages in 5 (sd05, sd09, dr07, st08, nl04); in one of them (dr07,
−14.5% as "about 15% below") the rounding made a day sound flagged. All 7 v2 failed answers quote
percentages to one decimal. The language check went from 48/50 to 49/50. English answered in Dutch
went from 2 answers plus 1 with Dutch fragments (st06, rd04, rd07) to 1 (st07), so rule 10 helped
but did not remove the problem. Passing answers are not printed, so this compares failed answers
only. The jump in automated passes from 28 to 43 is mostly the scorer fixes, not the prompt: the two
runs used different scorers, and 16 of v1's 22 failures were scorer false alarms.

## Haiku vs Sonnet (prompt v2, current scorer)

Automated: Haiku passed 43/50, Sonnet 39/50. After the audit, Haiku had 4 failed answers with a real
mistake and Sonnet 2. Answers with a wrong number: Haiku 1, Sonnet 2. Both miscounted the flagged
days in dr02 (Haiku 13, Sonnet 11; the true count is 12). Haiku's other real mistakes were language
and text quality (a Dutch answer, a garbled word, and a self-computed staffing number); Sonnet had
none of these. Sonnet's lower automated score comes mostly from scorer false alarms (7 of its 11
failures) and two unclear out-of-scope or tool-choice cases. Sonnet cost $1.5430 per 100 questions
against Haiku's $0.0888 (about 17×), with slower latency (p50 4.34 s vs 3.703 s) and shorter answers
(385.9 vs 534.1 output tokens). **Caveat:** this is 50 questions and one run per configuration, and
only failed answers were hand-checked. A difference of one or two answers is within run-to-run noise,
and these runs do not show that either model is more accurate.

## Scorer false-alarm patterns still open

Found in the two v2 runs (current scorer); the scorer was not changed in this report.

1. **Day-of-month numbers in short date lists** without a month on each item ("Wed 7, Sat 10 and Fri 16
   Jun", "Thu 15 and Mon 12") are checked as quantities. (Haiku dr03, dr08)
2. **Counts of days in other wording**: "Two other days are close but not flagged", Dutch "5 dagen
   drukker of rustiger". The count check only knows "N busy/flagged days". It works when the wording
   matches: it correctly caught "13" and "Eleven" in dr02. (Sonnet dr05, nl03)
3. **Threshold wording**: "within 15% of usual", "minder dan 15% af" are not recognised as the 15%
   threshold. (Sonnet rd06, nl03)
4. **Range definition and target wording**: "8 in 10 past days landed/fell inside", "built so that ... (80%)
   land inside it", "lower than 80%", Dutch "Het doel is 80%" are read as coverage claims. (Haiku ta02, nl05;
   Sonnet ta01, ta03)
5. **Day resolution**: "This Sunday" is resolved to the current week instead of the day under discussion
   (Sonnet sd06). A day named in a side remark ("If you meant Sat 10 Jun") is carried into the next
   bullets (Sonnet rd07).
6. **Decline wording**: "I don't know ... None of my tools provide ..." is not recognised as a refusal. (Sonnet
   oos06)
7. **Strict tool lists (design question)**: an extra read-only call whose output the answer uses (dr07
   `get_flagged_days` for the threshold), and sharing correct forecast numbers while declining a booking
   (oos07). Decide per item whether these should pass.

### Scorer misses seen in this audit (false negatives)

These defects were not what made the answers fail, so similar ones may exist in passing answers:
"Two are busy" in Sonnet rd06 (3 are busy) was not flagged. Dutch fragments in an English answer
passed the language check (Haiku v1 rd07). A wrong weekday label ("Ma 20 jun" for a Tuesday) is not
checked. Garbled words are not checked.
