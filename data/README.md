# Data

`hotels.csv.gz` is the hotel booking demand dataset, gzipped and otherwise unmodified:

> Antonio, N., de Almeida, A. & Nunes, L. (2019). Hotel booking demand datasets.
> *Data in Brief*, 22, 41-49. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2018.11.126

Licence: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Copy taken from the TidyTuesday mirror (2020-02-11), which combines the paper's two files (H1
resort hotel, H2 city hotel) into one CSV with a `hotel` column. 119,390 bookings with arrival
dates from 1 July 2015 to 31 August 2017. The authors removed all hotel and customer identifiers.
